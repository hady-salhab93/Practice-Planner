# Practice Planner

Language agreed during feature discovery for organizing guitar-practice material.

## Language

**Material**:
Something the guitarist wants to practice, such as a book, album, song, exercise, or passage. Material can contain smaller Material, has at most one parent, and has no completion status.
_Avoid_: Practice Item

**Library**:
The collection of Material available for planning practice.
_Avoid_: Backlog
